EXT.ESTATE PREMISES-MORNING: 8:15 AM An estate road dividing uniform rows of terraced houses opposite each other. It had rained the night before. Beat. From afar, BISI runs out of her house and down the road. She’s late for work. CLOSE ON: Bisi struggles to bring out her phone from her work bag. BISI C’mon, c’mon…. She finally pulls it out. Her hand is up high in triumph. Then. VROOM! A BLACK JEEP zooms past a puddle of water; it splashes and drenches Bisi. Bisi is in utter shock; then gets irritated. But she’s late! She sighs at herself and looks at the time. She has to go back. She makes a run back to her house. CUT TO: EXT.MANSION VILLA:9:15AM Bisi—donned in different work attire-SPRINTS down the luscious green driveway of her boss’ mansion. CUT TO: INT.MANSION-VILLA. People are scattered about being busy; THE MANAGER is addressing a worker over an issue on her iPad. Bisi hurriedly enters. The working atmosphere halts. The manager looks up. MANAGER (worried) Bisi. BISI I know. The manager watches Bisi take the flight of stairs. Her frown deepens. INT.STUDIO SPACE. A photo shoot is ongoing: A famous actress (BISI’S BOSS) poses flawlessly on set; a mix of poise, elegance, and rigor; lights flash everywhere; stylists rushing around; a makeup artist touching her up mid-shots. To the people on set, Bisi's presence is barely noticed. The photo shoot is over. Someone hands the actress a disposable cup of water as she steps away from the set. Bisi quickly approaches. BISI (apologetic) Ma’am, I-- The actress sips from the cup and hands it to Bisi. ACTRESS (cold and calm) Throw it away. I won’t be needing that anymore. Bisi freezes, cup in hand; The actress’ calmness unnerves her. Bisi goes away to dispose of it. Then. ACTRESS (CONT’D) And Bisi? Bisi turns. ACTRESS (CONT’D) That goes the same for you. (then: calling out someone else as she walks away) Jessica! Bring me another cup of water. Bisi’s face falls. She glances down at the cup; There is still water in it. Realization dawns. She’s been fired. EXT.CITY’S MAIN ROAD: 9:30 am The normal day activity permeates: Cars roll past; people go about their day. Then, there is Bisi down the road; her steps are slow, walking past people who don’t even bother to notice her. Part of her world just crumpled, people! CLOSE ON: Her WORK BAG hangs low over her heavy shoulder. Her phone on one hand. Then- Commotion ensues. A man runs past and brushes her. She stumbles backward-- Another man appears and snatches her bag away. She reacts. People don’t seem to care. She drops to a squat, about to have a breakdown. Then. Her phone rings. She brings it up to her face. CALLER ID: TEMISOLA. Her face crumbles. She picks. BISI (about to cry) Temmmi- Suddenly, her phone is snatched from her hand right there. She’s left in utter shock, her jaw hanging like ‘AHHHHH’. CUT TO: EXT. APARTMENT COMPLEX: 10 am An upper-class apartment complex. Bisi rings the doorbell to an apartment. LONG PAUSE. The door swings open. DARREN stands in subtle shock. Bisi gently pushes past him. INT.DARREN’S APARTMENT- CONTINUOUS and walks into his apartment; A Bachelor’s Pad. A giant glass-framed magazine cover of him, donned in a Luxury fashion brand-MAISON NOIRE’s latest collection. Other photos of him in Maison Noire’s collection hang everywhere. DARREN (looks inconvenienced) Bisola, what are you doing here? BISI (already in tears) Babe, you won’t believe what happened. She paces back and forth in his living room. He moves to her. DARREN Bisola. BISI (trying to control her tears) My morning has been horrible. First, my alarm didn’t go off this morning, which, by the way, never happens- DARREN (looks towards the back and then back at her) Bisola, we need to talk. BISI
